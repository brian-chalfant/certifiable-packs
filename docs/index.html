Certifiable

Practice questions, mock exams and study plans for IT certification exams from CompTIA, Microsoft, ISC2 and EC-Council.

Support

Found a wrong answer, a bug, or have a question? Open an issue at github.com/brian-chalfant/certifiable-packs/issues. For a question problem, include the exam and a few words of the question.

Common questions

Are these the real exam questions?

No. They are original practice questions written to the published exam objectives, plus openly licensed questions credited in the app. Real exam questions are confidential and are never used.

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your device. To move it to another device, use Export progress in Settings and import the file on the other device.

Does the app need the internet?

No. Every exam is built into the app. When online, it downloads updated question packs in the background.

What do the coloured dots on exams mean?

They rate how complete each question bank is: green covers every exam objective well, yellow has some gaps, red is still thin. Tap an exam to see the reasons.
