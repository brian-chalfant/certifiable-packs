Certifiable

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Certifiable is a study app for IT certification exams. It is built to work without knowing who you are.

What we collect

Nothing. Certifiable has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your study progress (answers, scores, exam dates, bookmarks, notes on passed exams and reminder settings) is saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage. It is not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it. Your device's own backup (for example iCloud Backup) may include it, under your device settings and Apple's privacy policy.

You can export your progress to a file from Settings. That file goes only where you choose to save or send it.

Question pack updates

The app checks for updated question packs by downloading files from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). These requests contain no information about you or your progress. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Reminders

Study reminders are scheduled on your device as local notifications. No notification service or server is involved.

Links

Some screens link to certification vendors' websites (for example renewal information). Those open in your browser and are covered by those sites' own privacy policies.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/brian-chalfant/certifiable-packs/issues.
